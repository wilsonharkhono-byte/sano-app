03 The Studio

Global perspective.
Local intelligence.

WHA Studio is a multidisciplinary practice working across architecture, interior design, construction coordination, and bespoke furniture production. Trained internationally and building in Indonesia, the studio pairs design ambition with a working knowledge of how things actually get made — on site and in the workshop.

The design-build advantage

Because the studio coordinates construction and fabricates its own furniture and built-ins, a detail drawn in the studio is a detail the studio can stand behind on site — one line carried from sketch to workshop to building.

04 Ideas

  1. I.

    Light on narrow ground

    On a six-metre-wide plot, the section — not the plan — decides whether a building breathes. Cutting daylight down through four storeys, as tested at the Expat Roasters flagship.

  2. II.

    The language of harmony

    Design that enhances the well-being of the people inside it while respecting the balance of the place around it — the studio's founding position, restated in every brief.

  3. III.

    Drawn, then built

    Ongoing research into closing the gap between drawing and construction in Indonesia — details designed with the workshop, not handed over to it.
